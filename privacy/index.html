Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Discipler is built to support mentorship within churches and ministries. We take your privacy seriously and keep things simple and transparent.

01

What we collect

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Availability and mentorship preferences
  • Basic profile information, such as interests, goals, and ministry participation
  • Mentorship activity, including meeting check-ins, responses, and follow-up notes
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and improve the platform

02

How we use your information

  • Match mentors and mentees
  • Send reminders, check-ins, and follow-ups, including SMS messages
  • Help ministry leaders understand engagement and program health
  • Support, operate, secure, and improve the Discipler platform

03

SMS communication

  • When you provide your mobile phone number and opt in, Discipler may send text messages related to your church or ministry mentorship program.
  • You opt in through your ministry's mentorship intake form, where you enter your own mobile number and check a consent box that is unchecked by default. A sample of that form is available at trydiscipler.com/sms-opt-in.
  • Messages are sent by Discipler LLC on behalf of the church or ministry running your mentorship program.
  • Messages may include meeting reminders, weekly check-ins, follow-up questions, and program coordination.
  • Message frequency varies but is typically 3–5 messages per week during active mentorship programs (up to approximately 24 messages per month).
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or of participation in your mentorship program.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. You can opt back in by replying START.
  • You can request help by replying HELP or by contacting us at support@trydiscipler.com.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

04

Who sees your information

  • Your church or ministry leaders and administrators, as needed to manage the mentorship program
  • Discipler, as needed to operate, support, and improve the platform
  • Trusted service providers, such as SMS delivery providers, only as needed to provide the service

05

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not send spam
  • We do not share your information outside your church or ministry context except as needed to operate the platform
  • We do not share mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent for marketing or promotional purposes

06

Your control

  • You can request to update or delete your information at any time
  • You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP
  • You can contact your church or ministry administrator with questions about your mentorship program information
  • You can contact Discipler at support@trydiscipler.com with privacy questions

07

Data security

  • We use reasonable safeguards to protect information submitted through Discipler
  • No system can guarantee perfect security, but we work to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure

Contact

Questions?

Reach out directly with any questions about this policy or your data.
