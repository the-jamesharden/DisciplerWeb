Questions about pricing

What happens if I go over my limit?

Don’t worry, nothing bad happens. We’ll let you know and give you two weeks to move up a size. If you haven’t by then, we’ll send one last reminder and give you a call. We never turn off a church’s mentorship for going over a limit.

Growing past your size does not change your price by itself.

Who counts toward my size?

You pay for people in an active pairing or group, mentors and mentees together. Everyone still waiting on your Roster is free.

Each person counts once, however many pairings they are in. A pairing only counts once it has been accepted, and not while it is paused.

What do I need to start the free trial?

A card or a US bank account, added through Stripe when you sign up. Nothing is charged until your 30 free days end.

When is my card charged?

Your first charge comes when your 30 free days end, for the size and billing you chose. After that, you are charged each month or each year until you cancel.

Prices do not include any sales or similar tax that applies.

Will you remind me before my trial ends?

Yes. We email you 7 days before your trial ends, before anything is charged. If you cancel before it ends, you are never charged.

How do I cancel?

Any Admin can cancel from Billing, at any time. If you cancel during your free trial, you are never charged. If you cancel later, Discipler keeps running until the end of the month or year you have already paid for.

After that, check-ins stop and your pages turn read-only. Your information is kept for 12 months, so you can restart from the same account at the normal price, with no second trial.

Are there refunds?

No. Cancelling does not refund any part of a month or year you have already paid for, unless the law requires it.

That is why the first 30 days are free: you can try Discipler with your people before you pay anything.

What if we have more than 1,000 people?

Book a call with James, and we will work out a price for your church together.

If you are on Up to 1,000 and grow past it, nothing stops and your price does not change by itself. We will reach out to book a call.

Is there an offer for church plants?

Yes. A church that launched in the last 3 years, or is launching in the next 12 months, can apply for 12 months free, which begins after its 30-day free trial. Answer Yes to “Is your church a church plant?” when you sign up.

Your trial starts straight away, and we email you when your plant is approved. We decide each application ourselves, and the free year ends if your subscription ends.
